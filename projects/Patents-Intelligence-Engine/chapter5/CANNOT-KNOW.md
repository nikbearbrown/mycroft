Things no model, and no market chatter, can tell me. My sources of truth are filings and the options chain — not tweets, not hype, not a fluent AI paragraph. Written by me, not generated, because the whole point is that this charter has to come from my own accountability, not a model's guess at it.

I am not treating Q3 or Q4 2026 results as a verdict on their own.

Not yet fully defensible on *why the stock specifically dropped 13%* — that link is still unverified (see STM.md claim 5 and the open sector-comparison check).
- **What specific evidence, if it resolved against the thesis, would make me exit — decided in advance.** Q3 2026 gross margin (reports ~Sept 26, 2026): if it comes in **at or above 37%**, that weakens my thesis — margin pressure easing faster than expected. If it comes in **below 35%**, that confirms real deterioration. Decided now, before the print, specifically so a bad number can't be explained away in the moment as "just noise, I'm looking at 2027 anyway."
- **Whether an options "signal" I'm looking at is real institutional positioning or retail 0DTE noise.** No model has the market-microstructure context to answer this reliably — it requires me actually knowing this options chain, this stock's typical volume, and this expiration's open interest.
- **My own psychological state around this position** — whether I am underwriting the thesis or underwriting my own prior conviction. A model cannot see this and will not flag it.

**Sources of truth (fixed, not situational):**
- 10-K / 10-Q filings, read directly
- Earnings call transcripts, read directly
- The actual options chain (term structure, OI, IV) for the specific expirations in question

**Explicitly not sources of truth:** social media sentiment, financial media headlines, AI-generated summaries treated as fact, price action alone as a "signal."
