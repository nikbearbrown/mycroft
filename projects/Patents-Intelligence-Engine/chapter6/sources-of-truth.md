# Sources of Truth — STM Desk

Ranked list of what counts as a source of truth versus context versus not-a-source, for this desk. Built from what has actually been used in `theses/STM.md` and `effort-plan-STM.md` — nothing invented.

## Source of truth
- **STMicroelectronics official Q2 2026 press release** (the earnings release itself, via ST's investor relations site) — this is what claim 1 (revenue beat) and claim 3 (margin drag confirmation) in `theses/STM.md` actually tie to.
- **STM Q2 2026 earnings call transcript** (July 23, 2026, Geneva) — management's own statements about the manufacturing-reshaping cost drag and Q3 guidance came from here.
- **STM's own SEC/regulatory filings (20-F, 6-K)** — not yet pulled for this desk, but this is the tier they belong in once retrieved. [NEEDS HUMAN: none pulled yet — this is a real gap, not an oversight to hide.]
- **Real-time or end-of-day options chain data from a named provider** (e.g., CBOE, a brokerage's chain view) — not yet pulled for STM. No options thesis exists yet for this ticker.

## Context only
- News coverage reporting on the earnings reaction (e.g., Investing.com's report of the 13% premarket drop) — useful for establishing that the drop happened and roughly why observers think so, but it is not itself a primary source; it's someone else's summary of primary sources.
- Same-day price-action reporting on other semiconductor names (AMD's 3.85% move, Intel's 12.44% move) — real, but secondhand reporting, not a raw price feed I pulled myself.

## Not a source
- Social media sentiment or commentary about "the options market screaming recovery" (this was the seed example's own framing, called out explicitly as unsupported in `theses/STM.md`, claim 2).
- Any AI-generated paragraph (including this one) — none of this is evidence on its own; it only counts once each claim is traced back to the tiers above.

**NEEDS HUMAN:** I have not yet pulled STM's actual SEC filings or a real options chain — the "source of truth" tier for both is currently aspirational, not yet exercised. Treat any future STM claims about filings or options positioning as unsupported until those are actually retrieved.
