## A discrepancy this pack surfaced, not resolved

Two different sources report STM's Q3 2026 guidance differently:
- One source (Yahoo Finance/Zacks, via the Q2 earnings article): revenue guidance of **$3.70B at the midpoint**, ±350bps, with gross margin guided at **37% ±200bps**.
- A different source (AlphaStreet): revenue guidance range of **$3.52B to $3.88B**.

These may both be accurate depending on rounding/methodology (a $3.70B midpoint with a range could produce a $3.52-3.88B band, roughly ±5%, which is plausible given "±350bps" language elsewhere) — but this pack does not resolve that; it flags it. **This directly matters** because the Q3 gross-margin threshold set in `CANNOT-KNOW.md` (≥37% weakens thesis, <35% confirms) depends on knowing the actual guided midpoint and range precisely. If the real range is wider than assumed, the thesis's exit signal may need recalibrating before Q3 actually reports.
