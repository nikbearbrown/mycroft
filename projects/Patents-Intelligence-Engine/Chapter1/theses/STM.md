## What the data actually shows, unfiltered

Two things are simultaneously true and your thesis needs to hold both:

- **Deterioration signal:** Management confirmed the manufacturing-reshaping cost drag is not temporary — it will persist "at similar level over the rest of the year," plus underloading charges (especially from new China fab startup) will not improve meaningfully in Q4, plus Q3 operating expenses are expected to *rise*.
- **Improvement signal:** Gross margin still improved 130bps YoY overall this quarter. Operating income swung from a **loss** of $133M a year ago to **positive** $187M. Data center revenue guidance was raised (above $1B in 2026, above $2B in 2027). Management confirmed sequential gross margin improvement is still expected into Q3 and Q4.

## NEEDS HUMAN

- Whether a 60bps non-recurring drag inside an overall-improving margin picture is enough to call the beat "masked" — that's a threshold judgment only you can set, and it should probably be set *before* you look at the stock price reaction, not after (otherwise you're reasoning backward from the drop to justify it).
- Whether the 13% drop is actually about margins at all, or about something else entirely (sector-wide moves, guidance vs. whisper numbers, valuation reset) — I could not confirm the market's actual reasoning from what's public here.
- Your own timeframe: the margin drag is explicitly described as persisting through 2026 — is your thesis about the next quarter, or about whether this is a multi-year structural issue?
