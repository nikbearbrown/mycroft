**Correction to the draft note:** the claim that "no dollar impact is quantified in the disclosure" is **not accurate** and has been corrected below. Item 3, Legal Proceedings, quantifies dollar amounts for numerous specific matters, and Item 1A gives an aggregate exposure estimate.

Confirmed detail (Item 3, Legal Proceedings):
- FTC 2020 modified consent order: $5.0 billion penalty (already paid, prior period).
- Consumer Privacy User Profile Litigation: $725 million settlement, became final May 14, 2025.
- IDPC (Ireland) GDPR fine over EEA-to-US data transfers: €1.2 billion administrative fine (under appeal, stayed by Irish High Court).
- European Commission Facebook Marketplace/DMA-related antitrust fine: approximately €798 million (appealed Jan 28, 2025).
- European Commission "subscription for no ads" DMA non-compliance fine: €200 million (appealed July 4, 2025).
- Spain unfair-competition case (AMI v. Meta Ireland): damages award of approximately €542 million (Nov 19, 2025 judgment, appealed).
- FTC v. Meta antitrust case (Instagram/WhatsApp divestiture sought): court ruled in Meta's favor Nov 18, 2025; FTC filed notice of appeal Jan 20, 2026.
- Aggregate exposure statement (Item 3): "The maximum aggregate monetary damages or penalties sought across our various legal proceedings could amount to an aggregate of up to hundreds of billions of dollars."

Also confirmed: ongoing antitrust actions and EU Digital Markets Act matters are disclosed as characterized in the claim audit, including DSA proceedings on youth safety and content moderation, and continued FTC administrative proceedings on the consent order.
