Update to the "no stated ceiling" note: the 10-K does include forward guidance. In Item 1A, Risk Factors, Meta states: "our investments in Reality Labs reduced our 2025 overall operating profit by approximately $19.19 billion, and **we expect our 2026 Reality Labs operating losses to remain similar to 2025**." So management has signaled 2026 losses should be roughly flat versus 2025, not open-ended — this resolves the item flagged for confirmation on the earnings call.
