# Sources of Truth (META)

Builds on theses/META.md and effort-plan.md. Every number and signal used in this desk must tie to one of the sources ranked here.

## Source of truth

- SEC EDGAR filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K) for META Platforms Inc, filed under CIK 0001326801.
- Named options-chain provider: none. I have no brokerage or market-data account, so options-chain signals are out of scope for this desk until I open one. Any options item in BACKLOG.md or effort-plan.md stays unresolved rather than sourced to a provider I don't actually use.
- Named brokerage/portfolio-tracking source: none — book/positions.csv is a human-supplied paper portfolio (entry prices/dates and current prices supplied directly by the human, not fetched from any account), not a live brokerage feed. book/returns-vs-benchmark.md is now a populated report computed from that file; see book/positions.csv and benchmark/spy.csv.

## Context only

- Sell-side analyst notes and earnings call transcripts, useful for management commentary but not a substitute for the filed numbers.
- Financial news reporting on earnings results, useful for a pointer to which filing to open, not as the number itself.
- Yahoo Finance analyst-consensus estimates (finance.yahoo.com/quote/META/analysis) — a third-party aggregation of analyst estimates, useful as the "consensus" side of an earnings-surprise variance, but not a filing and not verifiable the way a 10-K line is. See evidence/consensus-estimates-Q2-2026.md. Re-pull before use; consensus drifts as analysts revise.

## Not a source

- Social media posts, price target tweets, and options chatter.
- Any paragraph generated by an AI tool, including this desk's own assistant output.
- Unattributed blog posts or aggregator summaries with no link back to a filing.
