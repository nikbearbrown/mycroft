# Hand-check — the answer key against printed debt tables

> Generated by `scripts/handcheck.py grade`. Each transcription (`manifests/handcheck/<episode>.json`) was read from the report's printed debt table, not from its XBRL tags, and graded *as a ledger* against the answer key. Agreement means the key, the grader and the printed report say the same thing.

## Calibration set (DESIGN.md §11's 10 company-quarters): 10 of 10 agree

The first grading of the calibration set agreed on **8 of 10**. Every disagreement was traced to
its cause, and each was a defect in the answer key or the grader, not in the printed report:

| # | Found on | Defect | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| P30 | Oracle 10-K | Repaid notes kept their original face amount tagged at the period end, with carrying amount 0; the key counted them as outstanding | An explicit zero balance wins; face at period end counts only when no balance is tagged |
