## Why precision fell: the false additions

Reading added 40 true bonds to L1 ledgers and 26 that are not in the target report — so recall rose and precision fell. The 26, read one by one after scoring (Claude's diagnosis, a judgment), are three kinds, logged for week 6 and not fixed against this run:

- **P57 — a proposed offering added as its own bond (10).** A launch announcement ("intends to offer $300 million of convertible notes due 2030") or a blank preliminary prospectus states no coupon yet, so its event has no match key and cannot merge with the priced bond that follows; the engine adds both. Oracle's blank five-tranche prospectus, CoreWeave's, Super Micro's, Cipher Mining's and Applied Digital's launches, and one of Equinix's.
- **P56 — existing notes read from a prospectus as new issues (10, all Equinix).** Two preliminary prospectuses' descriptions of the notes already outstanding; no amount was read for any of them. P52's set-aside covers only values read from table rows, and these were not.
- **P58 — the banks' option listed as a bond of its own (4).** "up to an additional $45 million" (Applied Digital), $75 m, $22 m (Cipher Mining), Super Micro's $300 m: part of the base notes, as in the answer key's own P38.
- One misread maturity (Digital Realty's euro notes as 2033 as well as 2035) and one coupon read as a new bond (Micron, 4.975% 2026).

All three main kinds are visible in the record (no coupon, no amount, or an option sentence), so they are rules for the merge step, not new model calls.
