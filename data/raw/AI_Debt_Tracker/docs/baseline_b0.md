# Baseline B0 — carry forward, scored on dev

> Generated by `scripts/score_baseline.py` with the grader in `src/ai_debt_tracker/eval/grader.py`. Every episode: `$AI_DEBT_DATA/week2/b0_dev.json`. Intervals are 95% bootstrap over episodes.

## What B0 does

Reads nothing. It takes the last report itemised finely enough for the episode's level, drops bonds whose maturity year is before the target quarter's year, and calls that the answer (`eval/baselines/carry_forward.py`). In 5 of 95 episodes the start report was too coarse and B0 walked back to an earlier, itemised one.

## Results

**L1 — per bond** (47 episodes, 587 true bonds):

- Bond recall: **90.8%** (87.5%–93.6%)
- Bond precision: **90.0%** (85.6%–93.8%)
- Principal accuracy on matched bonds: **99.2%** (98.0%–100.0%)
- Episodes reproduced exactly (every bond, nothing extra): **18 of 47**

**Change detection** (42 episodes whose start report is itemised too; 21 of them have at least one bond added or removed):

- New bonds caught: **0.0%** (0.0%–0.0%) of 35
- Removed bonds caught: **6.7%** (0.0%–21.1%) of 15

B0 cannot catch a new bond by construction; it catches a removal only when the bond's maturity year has passed. **This is the headroom:** every new bond, and every early repayment, is something only reading the filings can find.

**L2 — per offering** (12 episodes, 84 true units): recall **76.2%** (57.3%–87.3%), precision **79.0%** (61.7%–89.3%).

**L3 — totals** (every episode, wherever the target report tags the figure):

| Figure | Graded | Within 2% | B0 had no figure | Median abs. error where it had one |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total debt | 72 | 33 | 0 | 3.4% |
| Current portion | 31 | 7 | 3 | 19.4% |
| Due in 12 months | 45 | 20 | 3 | 6.3% |
| Due in years 2-5 | 15 | 2 | 11 | 1.8% |
| Due after year 5 | 17 | 4 | 11 | 0.4% |

Most reports tag the maturity ladder only in the annual report, so a carried-forward 10-Q has no ladder to offer a 10-K target: those count under *B0 had no figure*.
